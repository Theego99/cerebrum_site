# Cerebrum editorial commerce preview

Local concept for the October 2026 growth plan. The existing landing page and domain configuration are unchanged.

Art direction follows the owner's 4 October feedback: use the existing coloured-pencil logo's warm paper, rose, clay, ochre and blue. Preserve its handmade quality. Avoid dark-green or technology-product styling. `logo-palette.css` carries that direction, including subtle paper grain and lightly irregular picture mounts.

Open `commerce-preview/index.html` directly, or serve this repository with a static HTTP server. Uses the existing portfolio photographs and wordmark. Category filters, work-detail dialogs, language switching, email enquiries and the link to the existing Musucus shop work. Portfolio pieces have no invented prices, quantities, dimensions or material claims. Japanese copy is a draft for founder review.

This is not a live shop. No payment or newsletter collection is simulated. A native Shopify section and page template in `shopify-extension/` provide the next implementation step with real collection products, product URLs and stock states. Shopify theme upload, payment activation, catalogue configuration, tax/shipping policies and test orders require store access.

WebP derivatives in `assets/` keep this preview lightweight; the original full-resolution images are retained in the repository. Before launch: founder-approved copy, confirmed product metadata and image rights, policies, native checkout, collection/stock configuration, and a real mobile checkout/refund test.


The makers section includes musucus and otumn. (@otumn_) with their original public logos. The musucus logo comes from its BASE shop; otumn’s logo is the 150px public Instagram profile image, retrieved 2026-10-04. Replace with a higher-resolution original when available. No stock or products are attributed to otumn without confirmed catalogue details.
